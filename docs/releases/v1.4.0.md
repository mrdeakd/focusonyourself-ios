# v1.4.0 — App Store Release

## What's New (App Store Copy)

Check in without opening the app. The new home screen widget lets you log your mood in one tap, right from your iPhone — no unlocking, no navigating.

Your mood now lives in Apple Health. Check-ins are written to the Health app as State of Mind entries (iOS 18), so your emotional wellbeing sits alongside your other health data where it belongs.

Meditations are lighter and faster. Narration audio now streams from the cloud and caches locally after first play — so the app is smaller to download, and your sessions stay available offline.

Fresh content, no update required. Articles, meditations, and affirmations now update from our server, so you always get the latest without waiting for an App Store release.

Plus the usual round of improvements under the hood.
